# Puerto Rican Dominoes

A web-based Puerto Rican partnership dominoes game.

## How to Play

- **Players:** 4 players, two teams (partners sit across from each other).
- **Deal:** 7 tiles each from a double-6 set.
- **Start:** First hand starts with the double-six. After that, the winner of the previous hand starts.
- **Play:** Match tiles to the open ends. Doubles are played perpendicular.
- **Turn order:** Clockwise.

## Scoring

- **Target:** 500 points.
- **Hands 1-4:** Premium (100 / 75 / 50 / 25) + remaining pips from the other three players.
- **Hand 5+:** Remaining pips from the other three players.
- **Capicu (+100):** Win with a tile that matches either end.
- **Chuchazo (+100):** Win with the double-blank.
- **Locked board:** If no one can play, the player with the fewest remaining points wins, and their team scores the other three players remaining pips (plus premium on early hands).

## Controls

- Drag tiles from your hand onto the board.
- AI tiles appear in the queue — drag them onto the board.
- Use the rotate button on a tile to change its orientation.
- Yellow box suggests a spot for queued tiles.
